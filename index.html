We build Galilee.

Galilee is an offline Bible study and sermon note-taking app for iOS and Android. Open a note, start typing, and when a passage is named just type the reference — the verse appears inline and drops straight into your note. Three translations, bundled in the app. No account, no connection required, nothing leaves your device.
