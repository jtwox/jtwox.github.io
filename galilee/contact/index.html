Writing about a problem?

It helps us a great deal if you include:

  • Your device and OS version — for example, iPhone 14 on iOS 18.2
  • The app version, if you know it (Settings › About › Version)
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see

Please don’t send the contents of your notes or anything private: we never need them to help, and Galilee keeps your notes on your device only.
