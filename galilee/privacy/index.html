Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 September 2026

The short version. Galilee does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. Everything you write stays on your device.

We have no way to read your notes, because they never reach us.

1. Who we are

Galilee is a mobile application published by JtwoX (“we”, “our”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles information, and applies to the Galilee applications for iOS and Android.

2. Information we collect

None.

Galilee has no user accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth. We do not collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, location, contacts or any other personal information. We do not operate a server that receives data from the app.

3. What the app stores on your device

Galilee saves the following into a private database inside its own storage on your device:

  • Notes you write, including titles, content and timestamps
  • Verse highlights and their colours
  • Links between your notes and Bible passages
  • Reading and activity history shown in the Activity Log
  • Your settings — chosen translation, font size and display preferences

This data is never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app removes it.

4. Bible content

Every translation in Galilee — the King James Version, the American Standard Version and the Berean Standard Bible — is bundled inside the app itself. Reading a passage or running a search happens entirely on your device. Your searches are not sent anywhere and are not logged by us.

5. Photos

Galilee can turn a verse and your reflection into a shareable image. Two optional permissions are involved:

  • Reading your photo library — only when you choose a photo as the background for a card. The image is used on your device to compose the card and is not uploaded.
  • Adding to your photo library — only when you tap to save a finished card.

Both are requested at the moment you use the feature, and the app works without granting either.

6. Sharing

When you share a card, iOS or Android hands the image to whichever app you pick. From that point, the receiving app’s own privacy policy applies. We are not part of that exchange and cannot see what you send or who you send it to.

7. Links to other services

The Settings screen links to our Instagram page and to Buy Me a Coffee. Opening either leaves Galilee and takes you to that service in your browser. Those services are run by other companies under their own privacy policies. We receive no information about whether you follow those links.

8. Device backups

Your operating system, not Galilee, decides whether app data is included in device backups.

  • iOS — if you have iCloud Backup or encrypted local backups enabled, Galilee’s database may be included, as with other apps. Apple’s terms govern that backup, and we have no access to it.
  • Android — Galilee opts out of Android’s automatic cloud backup, so its data is not copied to Google’s servers.

9. Children

Galilee is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children. Because we collect nothing, there is no circumstance in which we could gather a child’s personal information.

10. Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase.

Your content stays under your control on your device. Delete a note in the app, or remove everything by deleting the app.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Because we hold no contact details, we cannot notify you directly.
